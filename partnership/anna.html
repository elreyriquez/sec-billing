Step 4

Deal structure

Recommended model: net revenue share (not immediate equity). You earn a percentage of net revenue from customers attributed to you — after payment processor fees, refunds, and applicable taxes where applicable. This keeps incentives aligned while SEC stays lean and in control during early growth.

0–50 10%

Term & tracking

  • Initial term: 12 months, then renew or renegotiate together
  • Tracking: referral code, affiliate link, or campaign page — assigned after you sign
  • Equity: not offered upfront; may be discussed later only if the partnership deepens significantly

Protections (formal contract to follow)

  • Written agreement with defined responsibilities
  • Termination clause and confidentiality
  • Brand usage permissions
  • Transparent reporting on attributed customers
